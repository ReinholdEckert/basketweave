Getting started

Do I need Apple Music?

Yes. basketweave is free, but it plays music from your Apple Music library, so an active Apple Music subscription is required on both iOS and Android.

Why is it empty — “It’s quite empty here”?

Nothing shows up for your child until you choose which playlists are allowed. Open the playlist editor with the button in the top right and switch on the playlists you want. If the button isn’t there, editing is locked — press and hold the lock icon and enter your PIN.

If the editor itself shows no playlists, create one in the Apple Music app first and tap Reload.

I can’t see any playlists in the editor

Make sure basketweave has access to your Apple Music (iOS: Settings → basketweave; Android: sign in to Apple Music again) and that playlists exist in your Apple Music library.

I added a playlist in Apple Music but it’s not showing

New playlists aren’t visible to your child automatically — that’s the point. Open the playlist editor (it refreshes your list) and switch the new playlist on.

How do I choose which playlists are visible?

Open the playlist editor and switch on the playlists you want your child to access. Everything else stays hidden. Details are in the parents’ guide.

PIN & parental controls

I forgot my PIN

There’s no “forgot PIN” option — that’s deliberate, so nobody can bypass it. Delete the app and reinstall it. This also clears your allowed playlists and playback history, so you’ll set those up again.

How do I unlock playlist editing?

Press and hold the lock icon and enter your 6-digit PIN. A simple tap only shows a hint — it’s a long press on purpose, so little fingers don’t open it by accident.

How do I change my PIN?

First unlock playlist editing (press and hold the lock icon, enter your PIN). Then press and hold the open lock icon again: basketweave asks for your current PIN, then lets you choose and confirm a new 6-digit PIN.

Can my child accidentally change playlists or settings?

Not once editing is locked with a PIN. For extra safety turn on kiosk mode, which hides the lock and edit buttons completely. See Kiosk mode.

How do I keep my child inside the app?

Use your system’s own feature: Guided Access on iPhone/iPad (Settings → Accessibility) or Screen Pinning on Android (security settings → App pinning). basketweave adapts automatically on Android; on iOS, switch on Kiosk Mode in Settings → basketweave.

Playback

Music stopped playing in the background

Check that notifications are allowed so the playback controls can stay active, and that battery optimization isn’t restricting basketweave. Some Android manufacturers are aggressive about closing background apps; adding basketweave to the battery-optimization allow list usually fixes this.

I get a “Playback problem” message

Tap OK and try again. If it keeps happening, tap Reset playback in that message — it clears the current queue and starts fresh. Also check your internet connection and that your Apple Music subscription is active.

Does basketweave work offline?

On iOS, yes: download the playlist in the Apple Music app and it plays in basketweave without an internet connection. Downloads need an active Apple Music subscription.

On Android, offline playback isn’t available at the moment — an internet connection is needed.

Why don’t songs play in my playlist’s order?

basketweave shows albums rather than song lists. It groups a playlist’s songs by artist and album, sorts albums by artist, and plays tracks in album order. A hand-made playlist order isn’t kept.

Some songs appear under “Unknown Album”

That happens when a song in your library has no album information. Adding the album name to the song in Apple Music or iTunes fixes it.

Android: “Your Apple Music sign-in has expired”

Reconnect when prompted: sign in with your Apple ID again on Apple’s screen. Your allowed playlists and PIN stay as they are.

About basketweave

Is basketweave available on iOS and Android?

Yes. It’s built separately for iPhone/iPad (iOS 18.6 or later) and Android (10 or later). Both work the same way and share this website and privacy policy.

Which languages are supported?

The apps and this website are available in English and German, following your device’s language.
