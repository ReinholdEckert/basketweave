basketweave Support

Thank you for using basketweave.

basketweave is a simple Apple Music player designed for children.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I see any playlists?

Make sure Apple Music access has been granted in iPhone Settings and that playlists exist in your Apple Music library.

Does basketweave require Apple Music?

Yes. An active Apple Music subscription is required.

How do I choose which playlists are visible?

Open the playlist editor and select the playlists you want children to access.
