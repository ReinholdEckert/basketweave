Set up once. Locked for good.

You decide what can be played — your child just taps and listens.

Connect Apple Music

Allow basketweave to use your Apple Music library. Apple handles everything — we never see your login.

Choose the playlists

Pick exactly which playlists appear. Until you do, nothing shows up for your child.

Lock it with a PIN

Set a 6-digit PIN so only you can change the selection. Then hand over the device.

Made for kids. Reassuring for parents.

A few simple ideas, done carefully.

Only what you allow

Your child sees just the playlists you’ve picked. No search, no browsing, no surprises.

A PIN-protected editor

Playlist editing is locked behind a 6-digit PIN. Unlocking needs a press-and-hold, so curious fingers don’t stumble into it.

Albums, not endless lists

Each playlist opens as a grid of album covers — easy for small hands to hit, even before they can read.

A player for little hands

Big artwork, play/pause, previous and next, and a simple slider.

Keeps playing

Music continues with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen or in the notification.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts. What you set up stays on your device.

Kiosk mode

A device that’s just for music.

Turn basketweave into a dedicated music player for your child. The lock and edit buttons disappear completely — nothing to tap, guess at, or try a PIN on.

  • iPhone & iPad: switch on Kiosk Mode in the Settings app, under basketweave.
  • Android: basketweave notices Screen Pinning and hides the lock and edit buttons automatically.
  • Pair it with Guided Access (iOS) or Screen Pinning (Android) to keep your child inside the app.
