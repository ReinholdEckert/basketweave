Privacy Policy for basketweave

basketweave does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app accesses the user's Apple Music library only after permission has been granted by the user. Music playback and library access are provided through Apple's MusicKit framework.

basketweave does not display advertising, use analytics services, or track user behavior for marketing purposes.


If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact basketweave.app@proton.me.
